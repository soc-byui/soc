Our Mission

The Security Operations Center (SOC) is the front line of defense in today’s digital battlefield. Our mission is to safeguard Rexburg City's and Madison School District's critical systems, investigate threats, and train a new wave of Cybersecurity analysts.

By joining our program, you will gain hands-on experience with real tools, incidents, and teamwork preparing you for the challenges of a Tier 1 and Tier 2 SOC Analyst role.

The SOC Training Path

The SOC moving forward will have two tiers of training paired with real world experience in the SOC. Everyone will be required to sign non disclosure agreements for both the City of Rexburg and the Madison School District prior to initiation of any training.

Those who haven't taken or are not taking Cyber375 (Security Analysis and Operations) for the Fall will start as Tier 1 Analysts. Tier 1 Analysts will also be paired with Tier 2 Analysts. Tier 1 Analysts will be allowed to start working immediately on tickets under the direct supervision of a Tier 2 Analyst or above, and are only allowed in the SOC to work or train while under the supervision of a Tier 2 Analyst or above. Tier 1 Analysts that log 40 hours of paired experience of time working in the SOC throughout their entire time at BYU will receive a letter of recommendation/certification of completion for Tier 1 Analyst for future employers.

Those who have taken Cyber 375, will take Cyber 375 in the Fall, or worked during the summer break in the SOC may choose to become a Tier 2 Analyst training consisting of being paired with Tier 1 Analysts. Tier 2 Analysts will work on evaluating tickets for second review from Tier 1 Analysts, and may come into the SOC to work or train without direct supervision. Tier 2 Analysts that have logged 40 hours of paired experience of time working with paired Tier 1 Analysts and reviewing tickets throughout their entire time at BYU will receive a letter of recommendation/certification of completion of Tier 2 Analyst for future employers.

We are in the process of developing our own training materials for the SOC, so be patient as I work on rolling out new training materials over the Fall semester.
