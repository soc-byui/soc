Our Mission

The Security Operations Center (SOC) is the front line of defense in today’s digital battlefield. Our mission is to safeguard Rexburg City's and Madison School District's critical systems, investigate threats, and train a new wave of Cybersecurity analysts.

By joining our program, you will gain hands-on experience with real tools, incidents, and teamwork preparing you for the challenges of a Tier 1 and Tier 2 SOC Analyst role.

The SOC Training Path

Paired training with mentorship with a Tier 2 Analyst and the HackTheBox – Junior Cybersecurity Analyst Learning Path.
